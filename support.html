Common questions

How do I unlock the rest of the game? After the first seven levels, tap a locked level on the map and choose "Unlock everything". It is a one-time purchase.

I bought the Full Adventure on another device. Tap a locked level, then "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Will I lose my progress? The game saves automatically, even in the middle of a battle. You can keep up to three separate save slots on the title screen.

Can I turn the music or sound off? Yes: pause a battle and use the music and sound toggles.
