Common questions

How do I unlock the rest of the game? Tap the gold "Full Adventure" button at the bottom of the world map (or any level with a gold key) and choose "Unlock everything". It is a one-time $2.99 purchase that unlocks all 21 levels.

I bought the Full Adventure on another device. Tap the gold "Full Adventure" button on the map, then "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Will I lose my progress? The game saves automatically, even in the middle of a battle. You can keep up to three separate save slots on the title screen.

Can I turn the music or sound off? Yes: pause a battle to find the Music, Sound, Shake and Haptics switches. Music and sound are also at the bottom right of the world map.
