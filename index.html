The Long Dusk has fallen

The Gloam pour out of the dark every night, and only the Beacons keep them back. One by one they have gone out. Tam, a lamplighter with more nerve than sense, sets out to light them again.

  • Light against the fog: Lantern Towers burn the fog away and reveal the monsters hiding in it
  • 21 levels across castle roads, deep forests, a stormy coast, the frozen north and the dark lands
  • Five tower families, each with two specialisations and power-ups
  • Heroes who level up, call down skills and hold the road
  • Boss battles at every Beacon, and monsters that keep coming until the boss falls
  • Lucky Moths, hidden critters, kill streaks and achievements
  • Your game saves on its own, in three save slots, even in the middle of a battle

Fair to play

The first seven levels are free. One purchase unlocks the whole adventure, forever. No ads, no energy timers, no loot boxes and no subscriptions.
