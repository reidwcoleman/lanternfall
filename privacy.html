Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Lanternfall is a single-player game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels won, stars, hero experience, settings and save slots) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

No tracking, no ads

Lanternfall contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It has no accounts and no sign-in.

Purchases

The Full Adventure unlock is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only asks the App Store whether the unlock has been purchased.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: brianpcoleman@gmail.com.
